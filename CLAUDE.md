# Writing rules

Apply these to every write-up, script, post, or other piece of content produced in this repo.

## Openings
- Never start a write-up, script, or hook with "Most [X]..." (e.g. "Most founders...", "Most people...").
- Never open with a sweeping generalization about what people think, fear, or believe.
- Open with a concrete scene, number, claim, or question specific to the topic.

## Facts
- Never invent figures, quotes, or results. If a source can't be read, use clearly marked placeholders and say so.
